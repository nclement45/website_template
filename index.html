Democratic Candidate Sherrod Brown Campaigns on Fighting High Cost-of-Living

By Nia Clement
Oct. 20, 2026

Who is Sherrod Brown?

Sherrod Brown is running to represent Ohio in the U.S. Senate in November's upcoming election. Previously, Brown represented the state in the Senate but lost reelection in 2024. Brown was born in Mansfield, Ohio, graduated from Yale University in 1974 with a bachelor's in Russian Studies, and earned a graduate degree in education from Ohio State University. He also served in the House of Representatives from 1975-1982 and as Ohio Secretary of State from 1982-1990. This November, the race between Brown and incumbent Jon Husted (R ) is especially close, with many considering Ohio a battleground state. Sherrod Brown’s campaign focuses on combatting the high cost-of-living overall. Within that he addresses topics like healthcare, data centers, climate change, and protecting veterans.

Healthcare

Sherrod Brown believes that health insurance companies should be held responsible. “It’s time to ban health insurance companies from denying the care your doctor says you need. And if they try to deny your claims, we’ll fine them,” according to his campaign website. Most recently, he hosted a roundtable discussion with community members on healthcare issues. He said that if elected, transparency would be a top priority so constituents can get the care they need and stop seeing big corporations make more money while they continue to struggle.

Climate Change

Data centers impact the environment, and while Brown has not provided updated, specific goals for addressing the environmental and climate consequences of AI data centers, his record shows support for environmental issues and climate change. In a 2024 article, it explained that when he sat on the Senate banking committee, he held a hearing on the economic risks of climate change and supported environmental protections and clean energy.

Data Centers

Ohio has been a hot spot for data centers since 2010, and Sherrod Brown plans to be tough on them. He is asserting that data centers will have to pay their utilities so residents bills don't Increase. https://www.sherrodbrown.com/our-fight/. In The New York Times. A Siena poll found that 3% of voters in Ohio said that AI and data centers were their top concern in this election. Voters in Ohio said data centers were their top concern this November; in other states, 3% more voters named AI, making this race in Ohio even tougher.

Protecting Veterans

Sherrod Brown met with constituents earlier this summer to share his goals of protecting veterans' benefits and, more specifically, putting people over large corporations. In 2022, he supported the Promise to Address Comprehensive Toxics (PACT) Act, which helps veterans get healthcare if they were exposed to toxins. Since then, over 32,000 Ohio veterans have received care.
